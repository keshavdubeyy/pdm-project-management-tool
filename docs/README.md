## The short version

- The programme runs a **28-week final project across Sem 3 and Sem 4**, with **12 dated deliverable checkpoints** in 7 named phases — documented only in a PDF.
- **22 teams, 4 mentoring lines, 1 coordinator** — and the coordinator is also one of the mentors.
- The expensive loop is **weekly** (meet → feedback → action items) but the visible loop is **every four weeks**, so problems surface a checkpoint late.
- The public archive keeps **title, summary, team and poster**; everything that made a project educational is discarded.
- **162 features catalogued, 40 Must-haves**, 95% of them grounded in documents we hold rather than assumption.
- The first build is the **milestone engine**, because everything else attaches to it.

## Evidence discipline

Every claim in these documents is tagged `[KNOWN]`, `[PARTIAL]` or `[ASSUMED]`. The project proposal commits us to assuming no solution before research, so a feature resting on an open assumption may be designed and prototyped — but is not promoted to Must-have until the assumption closes. Open questions are numbered `V1`–`V18` and each is assigned to a specific interview in Weeks 3–6.
